How I work: directing AI

I get this quality out of a stock model by directing the instance, not by hoping. An explicit operating directive, hard constraints (simulation only, no fabricated data, no claim without proof), and verification at each step keep the model on track and out of drift. The verifier above is that idea reduced to a number: structure takes hallucinated output to zero. It is repeatable on any capable model, no fine-tuning required.
