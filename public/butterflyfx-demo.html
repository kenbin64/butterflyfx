ButterflyFx runtime

Reproducible, auditable, tamper-evident state · live and interactive
Most bugs in distributed and AI systems come from one thing: state that is mutated in place, so you can't reproduce it, replay it, or trust it. ButterflyFx wraps any reducer and derives state from a seed and an append-only, hash-chained log instead. Click below and watch the guarantees hold.

The problem, and the fix

Problem: when you store and mutate state, you lose the ability to answer "how did we get here?" A bug you can't reproduce is a bug you can't fix, and in a distributed or probabilistic system that question is everything.

Solution: derive, never store. Keep one source of truth (a seed plus an ordered log of what happened) and compute every state as a pure fold over it. That single discipline makes state reproducible (same inputs, same bytes), auditable (time-travel to any point), and tamper-evident (the log is hash-chained, so altering the past is detectable). This is event sourcing, and the demo below is the real runtime, not a mockup.

Why you can trust it

Tamper-evident

Each log entry binds the previous hash, the action, and the resulting state. Change any past action and the head hash changes, so a trusted head proves the whole history. The "Tamper" button shows it: edit one past action and verification fails.

Reproducible

State is a fold over (seed, log). Same inputs give byte-identical state on any machine. Nothing is stored that cannot be recomputed.

Auditable

The log is the full ordered history. Time-travel to any prefix. Rejected actions never enter the record at all.

Open and tested

One small runtime, MIT licensed, covered by tests. The exact file powering this page is butterflyfx.js in the repo.
