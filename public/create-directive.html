Create a Directive
A versioned contract for an AI · built in your browser
A prompt is text you hope the model follows. A directive is a small, versioned contract: a single purpose, the entities it may touch, the operations it may run, the things it must refuse, and one governing invariant every output has to satisfy. Answer a few fields below and download a directive you can paste straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or your own agent. Nothing is uploaded; it all runs in this tab.
